About

Hi, I’m Irina. I work at OpenMined, where I lead ScreamingFace 😱. I’m interested in how we can build an AI ecosystem where power, ownership, and opportunity are spread more widely through attribution-based control. Before this, I was at Google and did my grad studies at EPFL.

Here’s what I find exciting: AI gets more capable when it draws on a network of models instead of a single one, and I hope our ScreamingFace efforts can systematically prove it. I’ve also explored how sensitive or copyrighted data can improve AI while respecting the owners’ terms around economic value, attribution and consent. Previously, I spent a few years building better tools for AI oversight.

People will only choose these alternative architectures if they’re at least as good as the status quo. I believe quality and craft are how we get there.

Outside of work, I love gastronomy and am still figuring out how to ride a bike (x_x).

What I’m building

  • ScreamingFace

    2026 — now

    Compositions of models already beat the best single model at the same cost. ScreamingFace is a leaderboard to see which routers and ensembles do, iterate on them and start using them right away with ease.

  • Syft Hub / Syft Space

    2025 — now

    Federated RAG infrastructure. You choose who your question goes to, and each recipient decides whether, and how much, to contribute their knowledge to the answer.

  • PySyft

    2021 — 24

    Tools to enable private, proprietary data access and evaluation, used in external research programs and AI oversight.

    • Christchurch Call Initiative on Algorithmic Outcomes

      The first privacy-preserving system of its kind for independent study of how algorithms on LinkedIn and Dailymotion shape what people see.

    • Secure Enclaves for AI Evaluation

      Pilot with Anthropic on safety evaluations that respect mutual secrecy for both external researchers and the company.

    • Reddit for Researchers

      Privacy-preserving access for academics to study Reddit communities, without the raw user data ever leaving Reddit.
